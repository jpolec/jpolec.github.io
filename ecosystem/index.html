QuantJourney / OneBook

quantjourney.cloud

An investment-decision operating layer for hedge funds, asset managers and sophisticated family offices.

OneBook connects research, portfolio state, risk, policy checks, approvals, order intent and outcomes into a replayable decision record. It is designed to sit above the systems a firm already trusts — IBOR, PMS, OMS, custodians, administrators and risk tools — rather than force a risky front-to-back replacement.

Traditional Tier-2 buy-side suites are broad systems of record that often require a long configuration or integration programme. OneBook is deliberately narrower: a governed overlay for the decision context those systems do not preserve — what was known, who approved it, what changed and what happened next.

  • A practical overlay for focused buy-side teams that need better controls without rebuilding their operating stack.
  • Same-book context for investment committee packs, pre-trade review, portfolio risk, exceptions and post-trade replay.
  • Private deployment patterns for family offices, hedge funds and investment teams with existing data and operational systems.

Quant API

api.quantjourney.cloud/docs

A managed, governed financial-data API for teams that want to consume data rather than operate a data-integration estate.

Quant API provides 540+ stable routes across 60+ data-source integrations and growing. It standardizes request and response contracts while QuantJourney operates provider selection, server-side credentials, normalization, cache and fallback policy, lineage and audit context. REST, the Python SDK and MCP use the same governed layer.

OpenBB is a strong choice for teams building and operating their own open-source provider stack. Quant API is the managed alternative for teams that want to outsource that operational work and call one tenant-scoped interface with metadata, controls and approved AI-agent tools.

  • Normalized routes, request IDs, provider metadata, warning propagation and point-in-time support where available.
  • MCP access gives Claude, Cursor, VS Code and internal agents scoped tools — never raw vendor credentials.
  • Enterprise engagements can include agreed SLA targets, onboarding, support, security review and private-deployment discussions, subject to the tenant and vendor terms.

Data Workspace

data.quantjourney.cloud

A financial warehouse and research workspace for teams that need lineage-rich fundamentals, portfolio data and analytical outputs.

The warehouse turns SEC/EDGAR and approved vendor inputs into versioned, point-in-time financial statements, normalized entities, derived valuations, factors and analytics. Restatements, source context and calculation lineage remain part of the data contract instead of becoming spreadsheet archaeology.

The catalog contains 600+ financial metrics, ratios and indicators across covered securities. Coverage and field availability depend on filings, source entitlement and instrument history; the service is not a promise that every issuer has every field at every point in time.

  • Financial ratios, valuation metrics, factor data and technical indicators ready for research, screening and portfolio workflows.
  • On-demand data products and integration work for funds, hedge funds and family offices that need an institutional financial warehouse without operating one alone.
  • Approved Data MCP tools for portfolio, risk, warehouse and reporting workflows.

Backtester

backtester.quantjourney.cloud

Python-native, reproducible strategy research that turns a hypothesis into an inspectable evidence packet.

It combines target-weight and order-aware workflows with walk-forward validation, crisis and regime diagnostics, execution-cost analysis and generated reports. In the published five-strategy native benchmark, its calculation core was faster than pm/bt, Zipline and LEAN on every fixture, and faster than Backtrader on four of five fixtures.

That is a defined benchmark, not a blanket speed claim: VectorBT remains faster for heavily vectorized parameter sweeps. QuantJourney is strongest when a team needs fast iteration together with execution semantics, diagnostics, source-controlled Python and a reviewable research record.

  • One local Python workflow for signal, factor, allocation and order-aware research.
  • Native walk-forward tooling, crisis diagnostics, 80+ research metrics and generated reports.
  • Validation reviews can examine data lineage, timing, bias, rebalancing, execution assumptions, costs and out-of-sample stability while code remains in the client environment.

Quant Interviews

quant-interviews.quantjourney.cloud

A senior quantitative-research review workbook for candidates preparing for quantitative roles at hedge funds, asset managers and systematic teams.

The public review edition contains 194 research questions across 14 chapters, two volumes and 1,600+ current PDF pages. It moves from mathematical foundations to production-grade quant systems: each topic can include derivations, intuition, Python, C++ or SQL implementation, tests, failure modes and validation.

It is designed as a rigorous bridge between CFA or CQF theory and the research-review, coding and mathematical reasoning expected in demanding quant interviews. It is an independent QuantJourney publication, not affiliated with CFA Institute or CQF.

  • Useful for quant research, systematic trading, data, risk and research-engineering interview preparation.
  • A free public-review edition with executable learning and verification material.

Common questions

What consulting work is available?

Jakub Polec is available selectively for quantitative and systematic strategy audits, investment-infrastructure architecture and implementation, and family-office or fund operating-model work. Engagements focus on technology, research process and operating controls rather than investment recommendations, discretionary management or custody.

How does OneBook differ from a traditional buy-side platform?

OneBook is an investment-decision operating layer, not a claim to replace every system of record. It works above an existing IBOR, PMS, OMS, administrator, custodian and risk stack to preserve the thesis, data snapshot, checks, approvals and outcome of a material decision.

Why choose Quant API instead of operating a provider stack yourself?

Teams can use Quant API when they prefer a managed, governed interface over running providers, credentials, normalization, policy, fallback, cache and audit infrastructure internally. REST, the Python SDK and MCP share the same tenant-scoped controls.

Does QuantJourney sell market data directly?

QuantJourney provides the software and governed data-infrastructure layer. Data-source access, licensing, coverage and availability remain subject to the relevant vendor agreements, tenant scopes and approved use cases.
