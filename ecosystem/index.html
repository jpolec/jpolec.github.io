What I help with

I work selectively with funds, family offices and investment-technology companies on the operating systems behind investment decisions.

Strategy audit & validation

Independent review of data lineage, point-in-time assumptions, leakage and bias, portfolio construction, costs, execution assumptions and reproducibility.

Investment infrastructure

Architecture and delivery for data flows, IBOR/PMS/OMS integration, portfolio and risk workflows, controls and reporting.

Data + AI integration

Controlled data and AI integration for investment teams that need usable tools without giving up governance or operational context.
