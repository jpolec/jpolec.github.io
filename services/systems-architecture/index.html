Investment systems architecture

Most investment technology problems are not tooling problems. They are sequencing problems - the data layer, the research environment, and the execution path were built at different times, by different people, for a smaller firm than the one that exists now. I design the target architecture and the order in which to get there.

What the engagement covers

Current-state map

Data flows, systems, manual steps, where the firm actually loses time.

Data layer

Vendors, storage, point-in-time guarantees, cost per marginal dataset.

Research environment

Reproducibility, environment parity, how ideas reach production.

Signal and portfolio construction

Where the logic lives, who can change it, how it's tested.

Execution and OMS/EMS integration

Order path, reconciliation, failure modes.

Risk, monitoring, and controls

What breaks silently today.

Build vs. buy vs. defer

Per component, with reasoning, not preference.

Deliverables

  • Target architecture document with component-level decisions and rationale
  • Current-state assessment and gap analysis
  • Sequenced 12-month roadmap: what to build first and what it unblocks
  • Build/buy recommendations with indicative cost and headcount implications
  • Two working sessions with your team plus one review round

Outcome

A roadmap your team can execute against and you can budget against - with the reasoning attached, so decisions survive the next hire and the next vendor conversation.

Recommendations are vendor-neutral. If a QuantJourney component is relevant, I disclose the overlap explicitly and treat it as one option alongside external or internal alternatives.
