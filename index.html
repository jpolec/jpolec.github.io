Quant Systems Architect & Founder of QuantJourney

I work with hedge funds, family offices, asset managers, and investment-technology companies on quantitative systems and the operating infrastructure behind investment decisions.

I also build QuantJourney, an investment-technology platform focused on institutional research, data, portfolio, risk, and AI infrastructure. QuantJourney is a technology business; it does not manage client assets or provide investment advice.

My background spans high-energy physics, Microsoft, technology entrepreneurship, Oracle, and quantitative finance. I have led technology and innovation teams across Europe and the Middle East, including as Head of Industry and Innovation, Director, ECEMEA at Oracle.

I have built three award-winning startups and received two global marketing innovation awards at Microsoft.
