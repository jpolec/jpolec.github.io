How I work

Research objective, operating constraints, data and implementation.

  1. Scope and requirements

    Establish the research objective, users, data and operating constraints. Define the expected result and how to evaluate it.

  2. Technical review

    Inspect assumptions, data quality, backtest design and system boundaries. Make the trade-offs explicit.

  3. Implementation and validation

    Turn the findings into an implementation or a focused technical review, with validation and a clear handover.
