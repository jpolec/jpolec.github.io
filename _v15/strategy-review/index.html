Strategy and backtest review

An independent review of a strategy before it takes capital - or after it stops behaving the way the backtest said it would. I look at the research, the data, and the code behind it, not just the equity curve. The output is a written assessment you can hand to an IC, an allocator, or your own team.

What I review

Data integrity

Sources, point-in-time correctness, corporate actions, survivorship.

In practice, this is where most reviews find their first material issue.

Lookahead and leakage

Timestamp alignment, feature construction, restatement handling.

Cost and execution model

Spread, impact, borrow, fill assumptions against realistic venues.

Many otherwise convincing backtests fail here first, at realistic size.

Statistical robustness

Parameter sensitivity, multiple-testing burden, out-of-sample discipline.

Capacity and liquidity

The AUM level at which the edge stops paying for itself.

Research-to-production parity

Whether the live system computes what the backtest computed.

Divergence between research and production code is common and rarely instrumented.

Deliverables

  • Written review (15-25 pages): findings, severity-ranked, with the evidence behind each
  • Reproduced backtest under my own assumptions, with the delta explained
  • Prioritised remediation list - what to fix before capital, what can wait
  • 90-minute walkthrough with your team

Outcome

You get a defensible answer to one question: does the available evidence support deploying capital, and under what assumptions? The review either provides independent evidence for proceeding within defined limits or identifies material weaknesses before they surface in live trading.
