The decision it supports

A written view of the evidence, the unresolved risks and the conditions for the next research or deployment decision.

Review coverage

Data and information timing

Point-in-time availability, universe membership, survivorship, corporate actions and restatements. Trace the path from observation to feature, signal, order and fill.

Research validity

Economic rationale, baselines, walk-forward and out-of-sample design. Purging and embargo where labels overlap; Deflated Sharpe and probability of backtest overfitting where the trial history supports them.

Robustness and portfolio risk

Parameter sensitivity, subperiods, market regimes, factor exposures, concentration, leverage and drawdowns. Resampling and permutation tests selected to respect the series structure and the hypothesis being tested.

Execution and capacity

Signal latency, order semantics, turnover, spreads, fees, borrow and financing where relevant. Stress participation rates, liquidity and market impact; estimate capacity ranges conditional on the execution model.

How the work proceeds

  1. Define the claim

    Agree the decision, strategy boundary, benchmarks and tests. Record what was tried before the final variant was selected.

  2. Reproduce the baseline

    Freeze code, data and configuration. Reconcile reported returns with positions, cash, trades and costs; log any result that cannot be reproduced.

  3. Challenge the result

    Run the agreed validation and sensitivity tests. Separate coding defects, optimistic assumptions and weak statistical evidence.

  4. Report and challenge

    Discuss factual findings with the research team, then deliver the independent assessment and a prioritised retest plan.

What I need from you

How the scope is bounded

Tests depend on the evidence available. Missing search history limits selection-bias analysis; simulated fills limit capacity conclusions. These limits appear in the report. Remediation coding and live execution trials are scoped separately.
