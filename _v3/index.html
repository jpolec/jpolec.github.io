03 How I work

Start with the
hard question.

A useful engagement starts with the decision your team needs to make and the constraint standing in its way.

  1. 01 / Define

    What needs to work?

    Establish the research objective, users, data and operating constraints. Agree on what a useful result looks like.

  2. 02 / Examine

    Where does it break?

    Inspect assumptions, data quality, backtest design and system boundaries. Make the trade-offs explicit.

  3. 03 / Engineer

    How do we make it hold?

    Turn the findings into an implementation or a focused technical review, with validation and a clear handover.
