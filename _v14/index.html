How I work

I start with the investment decision or workflow you need to support: who will use the system, what data is available and where the current process breaks down. We agree on the scope, constraints and how to evaluate the result.

I review the existing architecture and research assumptions, including data quality, point-in-time history, backtest design and execution costs. The findings determine what needs to change and what can stay.

My team and I implement the agreed changes, validate them against the requirements and document the system for handover. The engagement can be a focused review, a specific component or a broader build alongside your team.
