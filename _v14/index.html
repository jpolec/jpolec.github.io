Work with me

Contact me when you need an independent assessment, a clear technical direction or help turning investment research into a working system.

Strategy & Backtest Review

Before committing capital or taking a strategy into production, I review the research behind it: data quality, assumptions, portfolio construction, transaction costs and the risks of overfitting or information leakage.

You receive a written assessment of what the evidence supports, where the results may be misleading and which issues need further testing or correction.

Investment Systems Architecture

When research, data, portfolio management, risk and execution need to work together, I help design the architecture or assess what is already in place. I examine data flows, system boundaries and operational requirements, including how decisions and results can be traced and reproduced.

You receive an architecture proposal and a practical implementation roadmap, with priorities, dependencies and technical trade-offs made explicit.

Quant & Investment Technology Due Diligence

Before investing in a business, selecting a platform or committing to a major technology project, I independently assess the underlying technology, models and engineering practices. I examine whether the system supports its claims and what it will take to operate, maintain and extend it.

You receive a structured assessment of strengths, material risks, unresolved questions and recommended next steps.
